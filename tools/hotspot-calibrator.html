Kit Stage hotspot calibrator

Click an instrument in the list to select it (orange = active), then click-drag directly on the photo to redraw its box. Pre-loaded with the current in-app coordinates — just nudge the ones that are off. When done, hit Copy C++ and paste the result into KitStageView.cpp's defaultHotspots(). The separate ride:bell hotspot is selected initially so you can draw it over the exact bell area.
